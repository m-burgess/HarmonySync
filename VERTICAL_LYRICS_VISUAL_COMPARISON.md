## Volunteer Workflow

### Before
1. Load song → See 10+ slides
2. Click slide 1 → Shows 2 lines
3. Click slide 2 → Shows next 2 lines
4. Click slide 3 → Shows next 2 lines
5. Confused about which verse this is
6. Go back to check section markers
7. Navigate through many slides

### After
1. Load song → See 4 section slides
2. Click "Verse 1" → Shows complete verse
3. Click "Chorus" → Shows complete chorus
4. Click "Verse 2" → Shows complete verse
5. Clear understanding of song structure
6. Easy to preview next section
7. Professional, organized workflow

## Key Advantages

### Structure Clarity
- **Before**: "Is this line part of the verse or chorus?"
- **After**: "[Verse 1]" clearly labeled at the top

### Navigation Efficiency
- **Before**: 8-12 slides per song, lots of clicking
- **After**: 4-6 slides per song, logical sections

### Volunteer Confidence
- **Before**: Memorize order of 2-line chunks
- **After**: See complete sections, natural flow

### Professional Appearance
- **Before**: Fragmented slide list
- **After**: Organized, section-based list

### Media Integration
- **Before**: Lyrics mixed with media, hard to find
- **After**: Clear sections, media easily identifiable

## Real-World Example

A typical worship song "Amazing Grace":
- **Before**: 6-8 lyric slides + 2 background images = 8-10 slides total
- **After**: 4 section slides + 2 background images = 6 slides total
  - Verse 1 (complete)
  - Chorus (complete)
  - Verse 2 (complete)
  - Bridge (complete)
  - Background image 1
  - Background image 2

Result: 33% fewer slides, 100% better organization!
